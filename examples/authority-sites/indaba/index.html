Rules & prohibitions

The system carries 4 rules, 2 prohibitions and 2 fallbacks. Rules and prohibitions are quoted; fallbacks are named. The rules are enforced by indaba-lint.

Rules · 4

RuleSeveritySummary
IND-1 · rounded-languagerule error Corners are always rounded — surfaces at 12-16px radius, controls and status as pills. Square corners are not part of this language.
IND-2 · palette-only-coloursrule warning Colours come from the Ubuntu palette and its tints: orange #E95420 (actions), aubergine #77216F (identity), deep aubergine #2C001E (destructive/heaviest), warm grey #AEA79F, warm tint #F6F4F2, cool grey #333333 (banner), text #111111.
IND-3 · text-not-pure-blackrule warning Body text is #111111, never pure black — black is harsh with aubergine, grey delivers balance.
IND-4 · status-word-firstrule error Status is a word first (pill with readable text); colour supports it, never replaces it.

Prohibitions · 2

  • prohibit/square-corners — Square or sharp corners anywhere — this language is rounded (IND-1).
  • prohibit/off-palette-colours — Colours outside the Ubuntu palette and its tints — including pure black, neon accents and off-brand reds (IND-2, IND-3).

Fallbacks · 2

  • fallback/platform-controls — Platform defaults for uncovered controls.
  • fallback/plain-region — Content without a defined composition sits in the shell as plain regions separated by generous spacing — softly, never in boxes.
