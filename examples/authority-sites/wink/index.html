Wink Interface System

pack wink · v0.2.0 (format 0.1) · snapshot: mailchimp.com live product + Mailchimp brand resources · derived from public sources, not a repository

A friendly, clearly-designed interface system derived from the Mailchimp brand and live product for the Design Authority synthesis experiment. Warm, pill-shaped, playful; no Mailchimp marks reproduced.

01 · Catalogue

What does the pack actually record?

Seventeen artifacts — six components, four guidelines, five patterns and two token sets — every one listed below with its own summary, exactly as recorded.

Components · 6

component/action-pill

Action (pill)

Primary action = pill, filled Cavendish Yellow #FFE01B with a 1px ink ring (box-shadow 0 0 0 1px #231E15 — live-measured), label in Peppercorn ink. On yellow/warm fields, primary switches to the dark variant. Secondary = outline pill (2px ink inset).

component/badge

Emphasis badge

Emphasis badges: pill, yellow fill, 12/600 label (e.g. 'Most popular' on the selected plan/category).

component/card

Card (soft vessel)

Content cards: white, borderless, radius 16 (24 for large), elevation via warm ink-tinted shadow rgba(35,30,21,.2) 0 8px 32px; interior padding 48. Tinted variant: Parsnip, no shadow. 1px borders (#DEDDDC) only on inputs/dividers, never as card devices.

component/field-select

Field & small-set select

Native select styled as a field for small fixed sets (≤ ~12): more rounded (12px) with serif field text per Gate 2 review. Larger or filtered selection out of scope; use the platform-controls fallback and mark it.

component/ledger

Ledger rows

Ledger: white surface, 16px padded rows, hairline #DEDDDC dividers (no zebra striping), header row in Parsnip with 13/500 labels; row hover = Parsnip tint; status pill inline; text links in ink with underline.

component/navbar

Top bar

Top bar: white background; dark links; dropdown items carry chevrons; hover = light-grey rounded-rect fill; primary CTA at right end; must stay single-row (no wrapping).

Guidelines · 4

guideline/hierarchy

Section rhythm

One page title; sections = small uppercase label → display headline → 1–2 sentence paragraph. Numbers may take display treatment for proof moments.

guideline/shape-language

Shape classes

Shape encodes class: interactive = pill (always with the 1px ink ring); content vessel = 16/24 soft-radius; structural chrome (bands, dividers, page blocks) = square.

guideline/typography

Typography (two registers)

Two registers: soft-serif display for hero/section headlines (with an italic emphasis fragment allowed inside a headline); grotesque sans for all body, UI, labels and metadata. Serif is the exception, never the working voice.

guideline/voice

Voice

Second person, question-first, plain English, non-blaming; dry wit allowed in microcopy; italic serif emphasis fragment allowed in headlines.

Patterns · 5

pattern/destructive-confirm

Destructive confirmation

High-consequence actions route through a rounded 16px confirm dialog with a consequence sentence in the warm voice; confirm = ink-filled pill labelled with the concrete verb (never 'OK'); cancel = outline pill; the destructive action never receives default focus.

pattern/dialog-overlay

Dialog (overlay vessel)

Dialog/overlay vessel: white 16px-rounded card on a warm ink scrim, instant appearance (no motion invented), content composed from existing primitives.

pattern/empty-state

Empty state

Empty states are a centered notice card: serif headline, one plain warm sentence, one primary pill action.

pattern/inline-notice

Inline notice (feedback)

Inline notices: parsnip-filled rounded block with a bold lead sentence and a plain supporting line; no toast pattern observed.

pattern/progress

Progress readout

Progress: yellow fill (#FFE01B) on a parsnip track, fully rounded; pair with a short step caption; no indeterminate motion.

Token sets · 2

token-set/colour

Colour roles

Roles: yellow = brand field + primary action only; peppercorn = text, ink, shadow tint; white + Parsnip = surfaces; Ochre = section band terminator; Kale = links inside content; #BF4055 = error text/fills.

token-set/type-scale

Screen type scale

Screen scale: display 48–64px w400 tight tracking (−1.2px at 64); section 35px/1.0; body 16/1.35; small 14; labels/buttons 13/500.

02 · Specification

What keeps a build honest?

Two rules, two prohibitions and two fallbacks are on record. Every quote below is verbatim from the pack; the two prohibitions were surfaced with boundary probes during the build, audited in log.md.

RecordKindOn record, verbatim
W-R1rule · error “Buttons are pills; no square primary buttons anywhere.”fix: Replace square corners on buttons with the pill radius.
W-R2rule · warning “Colours come from the wink palette only: Cavendish Yellow, Peppercorn ink, Parsnip, Ochre, Kale, #BF4055 error.”
prohibit/square-buttonsprohibition “Square or sharp button shapes — buttons are pills (W-02, W-08).”
prohibit/off-palette-coloursprohibition “Colours outside the wink palette, including pure black text and clinical cold greys (W-06).”
fallback/light-onlyfallback “No dark-mode canon exists; if a dark surface is unavoidable, keep ink/yellow roles and mark the improvisation.”
fallback/platform-controlsfallback “If the system defines no control for a need, use the platform's native element with the wink field styling (radius 8, warm ink text); keep native semantics.”

Probe signals that surfaced the prohibitions: “square corners” and “pure black”.
