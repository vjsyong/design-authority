**Second-pass tuning (post-build verification).** The first full run scored
31/33: two findings, both fixed at the curation layer, no kernel changes:
1. *"irreversible … typed reason"* resolved to the **guideline** (7.0 ≥ 6.5
   DIRECT_MIN) and never reached the recipe. Fix: the guideline keeps the
   reason-bearing case in its normative `do` list, but its `quote` was
   reverted to the canonical INTERACTION.md representative set — quote
   honesty *and* the phrasing now composes the recipe (guideline back at 6.0).
2. *"assign a reviewer with a searchable picker"* had flipped to
   `component/select` (22 vs cb 13 — two multi-word select aliases were worth
   +5 phrase bonuses each). Fix: select's `"assign picker"`/`"reviewer picker"`
   phrases became single-token aliases (`"assign"`, `"reviewer"`), and cb
   gained `"searchable picker"`. Result: `component/cb` (18 vs 12) — the
   frozen-golden semantic is preserved while all G-01 mappings stay sanctioned.
Also adjudicated: terse *"remove a reviewer"* is UNDEFINED on **both** 0.12.1
and 0.13.0-experiment — not a regression (tracked, no vocabulary contortion).
