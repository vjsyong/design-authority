## The read

The Economist reads as **editorial precision with a dry smile**: a red thread
through black-and-white restraint, typography as the primary instrument, and
rectangles as the recurring geometry of everything from covers to motion
assets. It is dense but ordered — headline, one-line standfirst, section
labels — with wit kept in the language rather than in ornament.

## Visual principles

1. **Red is punctuation, not paint.** Economist Red `#E3120B` (with 42/60/95
   tints) is the single accent in a black/white base — the "red thread" that
   ties everything together.
2. **Typography carries the identity.** Three registers: Serif for body and
   headlines; Sans for nav, metadata, captions; Sans Headline for display —
   contrast via weights and letterforms, never via colour.
3. **Rectangles are the geometry.** "Rectangles of varying sizes, static and
   animated" with four semantic registers (Lens · Steps · Frame · Stage) —
   the shape vocabulary is rectilinear and deliberate.
4. **Crisp lines over soft chrome.** Typing cursors, search bars, rules —
   interface cues rendered as sharp editorial marks. (No radius or shadow
   language is specified — carried UNDEFINED, rendered honest-rectilinear.)
5. **Less is more; unburdened by decoration.** Reduction over ornament
   (Marber principle, verbatim).
6. **Dense but legible.** Headline-led stacks; one-line standfirsts; section
   labels as wayfinding; the "world in brief" digest as the density exemplar.
7. **Imagery is purposeful.** Blur→focus photographic reveals; imagery adds
   context, never decoration.
8. **A grid with discipline.** Marber includes a "Web Grid" and patterns —
   symmetry and proximity as harmony tools (values carried UNDEFINED).
9. **Intelligence and wit.** "Clear but not dry" — provocation and delight
   are allowed; hollowness is not.
10. **Recognisable consistency.** Same standard as the journalism, across
    every medium.

## Typographic character

- Serif = voice (body, descriptions, headlines; Light→Black + italics).
- Sans = apparatus (section openers, fly titles, datelines, metadata,
  captions).
- Sans Headline = display only (covers).
- Fallback-driven stand-ins for the tile (their own declared stacks):
  Gelasio ≈ `Georgia` (declared serif fallback), Inter ≈
  `system-ui/Helvetica` stack, Archivo Black ≈ `'Arial Black'` display
  fallback. Commercial faces are not vendored; substitutes are declared on
  the tile.
- Hierarchy is family/weight contrast, not size theatre; standfirsts are
  terse, single lines.

## Shape language

Rectilinear throughout: rectangles (varying sizes, four semantic registers),
crisp rules, terminal glyphs "reborn as a visual language" (rhythm marks).
Corners/shadows: UNDEFINED in sources → the tile renders honest 0-radius
geometry rather than inventing softness.

## Density

High-information, low-noise: dense headline groups with thin gutters and
one-line deks; section labels as landmarks; scans fast, reads authoritative.

## Surface treatment

Black/white base surfaces with red used as block punctuation; crisp rules as
dividers; tinted canvas washes (95-set) for section separation. No elevation
language specified (UNDEFINED).

## Imagery treatment

Purposeful photography: blur→focus reveals as a sectional transition device;
video modules; imagery supports story, never decoration.

## Layout rhythm

Section label → headline + one-line standfirst, repeated in disciplined
groups; "The world in brief" digest module; weekly Edition block; covers as
the display stage for the Headline face.

## Motion character

Motion is native to the identity (static + animated rectangles; typing
cursors; motion design in the 2025 refresh scope). Durations/easing
UNDEFINED — carried.

## Visual hierarchy

Serif/sans role contrast; red as the single attention signal; display face
reserved for covers; metadata quietly whispered (small caps-ish sans, greys).

## What leader would avoid (from the evidence)

Decoration for its own sake; excessive or arbitrary styles; colour as
hierarchy; soft blobby chrome; hollow hype; dryness without wit.

## Carried UNDEFINEDs (deliberate)

Numeric type scale · spacing/grid values · corner radii · shadow/elevation ·
feedback & status patterns · hover/motion timings · breakpoints · live-2026
nav specifics.
