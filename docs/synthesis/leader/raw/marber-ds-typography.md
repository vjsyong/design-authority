# Typography

Our typographic system provides a selection of font sizes and reusable type styles built upon the unique character of our bespoke typefaces.

## The Economist

*The Economist* has two typefaces that form the visual core of everything published across digital, print, social, video and marketing: **Economist Serif** and **Economist Sans**. Both typefaces have been custom designed for our content and draw on our rich history as a newspaper of 180 years.

### Economist Serif

Primarily for body text, descriptions and headlines.

Includes: *Light, Light Italic, Regular, Regular Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, Semibold, SemiBold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, ExtraBold, ExtraBold Italic, Black, Black Italic.*

| **Name** | **Value** | **iOS/Android** | **CSS** | 
|  | Economist Serif |  | --mb-typeface-serif | 

Font fallbacks

'EconomistSerif', ui-serif, Georgia, Times, 'Times New Roman', serif

### Economist Sans

Primarily for navigation elements, such as: section openers, fly titles, datelines, metadata, captions.

Includes: *Light, Light Italic, Regular, Regular Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, Semibold, SemiBold Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, ExtraBold, ExtraBold Italic, Black, Black Italic*.

| **Name** | **Value** | **iOS/Android** | **CSS** | 
|  | Economist Sans |  | --mb-typeface-sans | 

#### Font fallbacks

'EconomistSans', system-ui, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif

### Economist Sans Headline

For use in display headlines.

Includes: *Regular, Medium, Bold, ExtraBold, Black.*
