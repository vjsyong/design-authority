The measure & the frame

Everything is measured from one master object: clear space = the height of the master’s “C”; signatures size at a fixed ratio. No absolutes anywhere — the system carries its own scale.

Motion, gated

Motion exists only where a product earns it, and must always resolve to the static approved form. The one timed asset is sonic: four notes of O Canada, 1.5 seconds. Nothing animates decoratively.

Gestalt axes (for review)

Corners square, no radius language anywhere Contrast posture sober ink-on-paper; red strictly ceremonial Marker shapes flag-red rectangles, rules, measured space Palette warmth cool and institutional Density austere — generous margins, structured hierarchy
