# Size and position

## Dos and don'ts
Do:
- display the Canada wordmark and a signature as 2 distinct elements
- place them on a horizontal baseline, never on an angle or their side
- display them in generous, open space
Don't:
- alter them in any way
- place them on a visually conflicting background
- use them as part of a headline, phrase or sentence
- skew, stretch or compress them
- translate them into a language other than English or French

## Sizing signatures to the Canada wordmark
Applies regardless of the positioning of the signature and the wordmark.
- Flag signatures: the height of the flag symbol in the signature is equal to the distance between the bottom of the wordmark and the bottom of the flag symbol in the wordmark. (2- and 3-line examples)
- Arms signatures: the height of a 2-line title in the signature is equal to the distance between the bottom of the wordmark and the bottom of the flag symbol in the wordmark. Same applies to arms signatures of 3 or more lines.
- Ministerial signatures: the width of the arms of Canada in the signature is half the width of the Canada wordmark.

## Positioning
There are 4 options for positioning the Canada wordmark and corporate signature in most products. The wordmark must align with the corporate signature in one of the following ways:
1. on the same baseline as the corporate signature which is displayed to the left
2. in the bottom right corner, with the corporate signature in the top left
3. directly below the corporate signature, aligned with the left-most text in the signature
4. directly below the corporate signature, to the left edge of the identifying symbol (the flag symbol or arms of Canada)
Certain products, such as stationery and videos, have specific requirements.
Ministerial signatures typically appear above the Canada wordmark; the arms of Canada in the signature is centre-aligned with the wordmark.

## Clear space
Minimum clearance between the Canada wordmark and signatures, and other graphic elements and text. More clear space is encouraged if the design of the product permits.
- Around the Canada wordmark: minimum clear space (x) = height of its letter "C", on all 4 sides.
- Around a flag signature: = height of the flag symbol in the signature, on all 4 sides.
- Around an arms signature or a ministerial signature: = half the width of the arms of Canada, on all 4 sides.
- When pairing wordmark + signature: the minimum space between them is equal, on all sides, to either the height of the flag symbol in the signature OR half the width of the arms of Canada.
