# Government of Canada websites

The official symbols must be applied to external-facing and internal-facing sites. The requirements also apply to web applications and to sites that are password protected.

## External-facing websites
The Canada wordmark and the corporate signature must be applied to all external-facing Government of Canada websites as follows:
- the Government of Canada signature appears in the top left corner of the header
- the Canada wordmark appears in the bottom right corner of the footer
- the Canada wordmark and the Government of Canada signature appear in:
  - black type with a FIP red flag symbol on a white background
  - Helvetica medium weight
These requirements are also in the Canada.ca Specifications, which sets out the mandatory design requirements for the government's web presence.

## Internal-facing sites
Internal-facing sites are accessible from a Government of Canada network only. They include intranets, wikis and blogs. The Canada wordmark and the corporate signature must appear clearly and prominently on all internal-facing sites.
